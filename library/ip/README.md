4 entries.


## Entries


### Omwaka alias Guardian Angel v Skysail Mabati Ltd (Civil Case E007 of 2023)
- **Citation:** [2026] KEHC 9529 (KLR) (12 June 2026) (Ruling)
- **Court/Forum:** High Court of Kenya
- **Year:** 2026
- **Source:** https://new.kenyalaw.org/akn/ke/judgment/kehc/2026/9529/eng@2026-06-12
- **Principle:** Copyright and related rights are protected under the Copyright Act; infringement attracts remedies including injunctions and damages.


### City Eye Advertising Agency v Mwenge Miraa Sacco Ltd
- **Citation:** [2022] KEHC 2083 (KLR) (23 February 2022)
- **Court/Forum:** High Court of Kenya
- **Year:** 2022
- **Source:** https://new.kenyalaw.org/akn/ke/judgment/kehc/2022/2083/eng@2022-02-23
- **Principle:** Copyright protects original works; infringement requires proof of copying of a substantial part of the protected work.


### Kenya Copyright Board v Music Copyright Society of Kenya (MCSK) & 2 Others
- **Citation:** [2022] KEHC 13176 (KLR) (30 September 2022) (Petition E276 of 2021)
- **Court/Forum:** High Court of Kenya
- **Year:** 2022
- **Source:** https://new.kenyalaw.org/akn/ke/judgment/kehc/2022/13176/eng@2022-09-30
- **Principle:** The regulation of collective management organisations must be transparent and accountable; the Copyright Act governs the licensing and distribution of royalties.


### Intellectual Property Owners Association v Kenya Copyright Board (KECOBO) & Another
- **Citation:** [2021] KEHC 4620 (KLR) (15 July 2021)
- **Court/Forum:** High Court of Kenya
- **Year:** 2021
- **Source:** https://new.kenyalaw.org/akn/ke/judgment/kehc/2021/4620/eng@2021-07-15
- **Principle:** The Kenya Copyright Board's regulatory decisions must be lawful, reasonable and procedurally fair; intellectual property rights are protected under the Constitution and statute.
