### Triple Point Technology Inc v PTT Public Company Ltd
- **Citation:** [2021] UKSC 29, [2021] 3 WLR 527
- **Court/Forum:** Supreme Court of the United Kingdom
- **Year:** 2021
- **Principle:** Liquidated damages clauses are construed by their terms; the entitlement to liquidated damages accrues up to the date of termination, not beyond.
- **Facts:** A dispute over liquidated damages in a software contract.
- **Holding:** Liquidated damages clauses are construed by their terms; the entitlement to liquidated damages accrues up to the date of termination, not beyond.
- **Judges:** Lady Arden (Supreme Court)
