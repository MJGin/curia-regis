3 entries.


## Entries


### Mark Kubai Kariuki & 2 Others v Attorney General & 2 Others
- **Citation:** [2015] KEHC 1527 (KLR) (28 October 2015)
- **Court/Forum:** High Court of Kenya
- **Year:** 2015
- **Source:** https://new.kenyalaw.org/akn/ke/judgment/kehc/2015/1527/eng@2015-10-28
- **Principle:** The right to marry and found a family is protected under the Constitution; restrictions must be reasonable and justifiable.


### G G W v R M W W
- **Citation:** [2004] KEHC 2654 (KLR) (18 November 2004)
- **Court/Forum:** High Court of Kenya
- **Year:** 2004
- **Source:** https://new.kenyalaw.org/akn/ke/judgment/kehc/2004/2654/eng@2004-11-18
- **Principle:** Divorce and matrimonial property disputes are governed by the Matrimonial Property Act and the Marriage Act; the court distributes property equitably.


### R N M v R C M
- **Citation:** [2004] KEHC 747 (KLR) (6 December 2004)
- **Court/Forum:** High Court of Kenya
- **Year:** 2004
- **Source:** https://new.kenyalaw.org/akn/ke/judgment/kehc/2004/747/eng@2004-12-06
- **Principle:** The best interests of the child are paramount in all matters concerning children, including custody and maintenance.
