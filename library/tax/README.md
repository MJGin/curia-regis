## Entries


### Commissioner of Investigations and Enforcement v Kanyago (Income Tax Appeal E069 of 2025)
- **Citation:** [2025] KEHC 15484 (KLR) (16 July 2025) (Commercial and Tax)
- **Court/Forum:** High Court of Kenya
- **Year:** 2025
- **Source:** https://new.kenyalaw.org/akn/ke/judgment/kehc/2025/15484/eng@2025-07-16
- **Principle:** Tax appeals are determined on the evidence; the Commissioner must prove the assessment and the taxpayer may challenge it on the facts and the law.


### Highway Furniture Mart Limited v Commissioner of Value Added Tax & Another
- **Citation:** [2015] KECA 566 (KLR) (3 July 2015)
- **Court/Forum:** Court of Appeal of Kenya
- **Year:** 2015
- **Source:** https://new.kenyalaw.org/akn/ke/judgment/keca/2015/566/eng@2015-07-03
- **Principle:** VAT assessments must be based on the correct application of the Act; the taxpayer bears the burden of proving that an assessment is excessive.


### Nakumatt Holdings Limited v Commissioner of Value Added Tax
- **Citation:** [2011] KECA 295 (KLR) (8 April 2011)
- **Court/Forum:** Court of Appeal of Kenya
- **Year:** 2011
- **Source:** https://new.kenyalaw.org/akn/ke/judgment/keca/2011/295/eng@2011-04-08
- **Principle:** VAT assessments must comply with the Value Added Tax Act; the taxpayer may challenge the assessment on the facts and the law.


### Commissioner of Income Tax v Westmont Power (K) Ltd (Income Tax Appeal 626 of 2002)

- **Year:** 2006
- **Source:** https://new.kenyalaw.org/akn/ke/judgment/kehc/2006/3474/eng@2006-01-30
