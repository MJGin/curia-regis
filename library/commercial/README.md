### Russia — Measures Concerning Traffic in Transit
- **Citation:** WT/DS512/R (5 April 2019)
- **Court/Forum:** WTO Panel
- **Year:** 2019
- **Principle:** The security exception (GATT Article XXI) requires an emergency in international relations, an essential security interest and a plausible connection between the measure and that interest.
- **Facts:** Ukraine challenged Russian transit restrictions on Ukrainian goods.
- **Holding:** The security exception (GATT Article XXI) requires an emergency in international relations, an essential security interest and a plausible connection between the measure and that interest.
- **Judges:** WTO Panel


### British American Tobacco (U) Ltd v Attorney General of Uganda
- **Citation:** Reference No 2 of 2017 (EACJ)
- **Court/Forum:** East African Court of Justice
- **Year:** 2017
- **Principle:** Differential treatment does not necessarily constitute unlawful discrimination; discrimination requires that like products be treated differently without reasonable justification.
- **Facts:** British American Tobacco challenged Ugandan tax measures before the EACJ.
- **Holding:** Differential treatment does not necessarily constitute unlawful discrimination; discrimination requires that like products be treated differently without reasonable justification.
- **Judges:** East African Court of Justice


### Canada — Certain Measures Affecting the Renewable Energy Generation Sector

- **Court/Forum:** WTO Appellate Body
- **Year:** 2013
- **Principle:** Local content requirements in the renewable energy sector are prohibited subsidies under the SCM Agreement where they confer a benefit conditional on the use of domestic goods.

### United States — Certain Country of Origin Labelling (COOL) Requirements
- **Citation:** WT/DS384/AB/R, WT/DS386/AB/R (29 June 2012)
- **Court/Forum:** WTO Appellate Body
- **Year:** 2012
- **Principle:** A measure that accords less favourable treatment to imported livestock and meat than to like domestic products is inconsistent with Article 2.1 of the TBT Agreement.
- **Facts:** The United States required country of origin labelling for livestock and meat.
- **Holding:** A measure that accords less favourable treatment to imported livestock and meat than to like domestic products is inconsistent with Article 2.1 of the TBT Agreement.
- **Judges:** Appellate Body

- **Court/Forum:** WTO Appellate Body
- **Year:** 2012
- **Principle:** Under the TBT Agreement, like products are those that are identical or similar in terms of their characteristics, end-use and consumer tastes, assessed as a whole.

- **Holding:** Under the TBT Agreement, like products are those identical or similar in terms of characteristics, end-use and consumer tastes.
- **Judges:** Appellate Body

### Japan — Taxes on Alcoholic Beverages
- **Citation:** WT/DS8/AB/R (1 November 1996)
- **Court/Forum:** WTO Appellate Body
- **Year:** 1996
- **Principle:** Like products are determined by competitive relationship, not by the measure's own criteria; likeness is assessed by physical characteristics, end-use, consumer tastes and tariff classification.
- **Facts:** Japan taxed imported alcoholic beverages at higher rates than domestic shochu.
- **Holding:** Like products are determined by competitive relationship; the Japanese tax violated Article III:2.
- **Judges:** Appellate Body


### Agreement on Subsidies and Countervailing Measures

- **Court/Forum:** WTO covered agreement
- **Year:** 1994
- **Principle:** A subsidy exists where a financial contribution by government confers a benefit; only specific subsidies are actionable, and prohibited export subsidies fall under Article 3.
