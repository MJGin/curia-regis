### Package Insurance Brokers Limited v Gichuru
- **Citation:** [2019] KECA 477 (KLR) (19 July 2019) (Civil Appeal 307 of 2018)
- **Court/Forum:** Court of Appeal of Kenya
- **Year:** 2019
- **Source:** https://new.kenyalaw.org/akn/ke/judgment/keca/2019/477/eng@2019-07-19
- **Principle:** Indirect discrimination is assessed by identifying the relevant policy or practice, its disparate impact, the disadvantage suffered by the claimant, and whether it is objectively justified and proportionate.
- **Facts:** Simon Gitau Gichuru, an operations manager at Package Insurance Brokers Ltd, underwent spinal surgery and was later suspended and dismissed, allegedly due to his disability and medical condition.
- **Holding:** The Court of Appeal found that his dismissal amounted to indirect discrimination and was unfair because the employer failed to accommodate his condition and provide a fair hearing. It partly allowed his appeal and awarded compensation.
- **Judges:** PN Waki, MS Asike-Makhandia and F Sichale JJA
