### Trustees of the Rural Private Hospital Association of Kenya v Chief Executive Officer, National Hospital Insurance Fund & 2 Others
- **Citation:** [2021] KEHC 3446 (KLR) (4 October 2021)
- **Court/Forum:** High Court of Kenya
- **Year:** 2021
- **Source:** https://new.kenyalaw.org/akn/ke/judgment/kehc/2021/3446/eng@2021-10-04
- **Principle:** Locus standi in constitutional petitions is broad; associations may sue in the interest of their members under Article 258.
- **Facts:** An association of private hospitals challenged the actions of the NHIF. The petitioners alleged violations of constitutional rights. The case raised questions of standing and association suits.
- **Holding:** The Court held that locus standi in constitutional petitions is broad under Article 258; associations may sue in the interest of their members.
- **Judges:** Mwita J (High Court)

### Samuel Kamau Macharia & Another v Kenya Commercial Bank Limited & 2 Others
- **Citation:** [2012] KESC 8 (KLR) (23 October 2012) (Application 2 of 2011)
- **Court/Forum:** Supreme Court of Kenya
- **Year:** 2012
- **Source:** https://new.kenyalaw.org/akn/ke/judgment/kesc/2012/8/eng@2012-10-23
- **Principle:** Jurisdiction is everything: a court must satisfy itself of its jurisdiction at the threshold; a decision reached without jurisdiction is a nullity. Jurisdiction flows from the Constitution or statute, not from consent or convenience.

### Speaker of the National Assembly v Karume
- **Citation:** [1992] KECA 42 (KLR) (29 May 1992) (Civil Application 92 of 1992)
- **Court/Forum:** Court of Appeal of Kenya
- **Year:** 1992
- **Source:** https://new.kenyalaw.org/akn/ke/judgment/keca/1992/42/eng@1992-05-29
- **Principle:** Where the Constitution prescribes a specific procedure or forum, that procedure must be followed; the court will not permit a party to achieve indirectly, through a different route, what the Constitution requires to be done another way.
- **Facts:** The Speaker of the National Assembly challenged a High Court order that had restrained Parliament from debating a motion. The dispute arose in the context of the transition from the former Constitution to the 2010 Constitution. The Court of Appeal was asked to determine whether the High Court had properly exercised its jurisdiction.
- **Holding:** Where the Constitution prescribes a specific procedure or forum, that procedure must be followed; courts will not imply a different route to circumvent the constitutional text.
- **Judges:** Gachuhi, Cockar and Omolo JJA (Court of Appeal)
